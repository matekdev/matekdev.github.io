Parkour Painters is a team-based multiplayer game where gangs race around the city tagging graffiti spots for points. It won **$1,600 USD** in an s&box game jam.

I built the networked parkour movement system: wall jumps, ledge grabs, and grinding. I also worked on core systems and input, and made one of the music tracks.
