Grubs is a multiplayer artillery game inspired by Worms. Each player controls a team of grubs on a randomly generated map and uses an arsenal of weapons to blow up the terrain and each other.

I was one of the main programmers. I worked on core systems, weapons, networking, audio, and UI. The game reached tens of thousands of players and was featured by [YouTubers with millions of subscribers](https://youtu.be/flcAvTeu1sw).
